# TALYNT LABS — knowledge base for the voice assistant

TALYNT LABS is an AI-native hiring agency in India. Clients buy a hiring service, run by TALYNT's team with Buddy, its AI hiring agent ("Buddy by TALYNT LABS"). All roles, across India. Pricing: a percentage of CTC, charged only when the client hires. After booking a demo, the team calls the client and walks through a live role.

## Questions and answers

**Who is Buddy?**
Buddy is the AI hiring agent by TALYNT LABS. It reads your JD, finds people, checks every criterion against the CV, and checks interviews against the JD. Our team and your hiring managers make the calls.

**Is TALYNT an agency or software?**
An AI-native hiring agency. Buddy is our agent; you work with our team, and you don’t have to learn a tool.

**How does Buddy read a role?**
Role insights turn your JD into skills, criteria and a ready search query, and flag vague or exclusionary wording. You mark the must-haves, and your hiring manager calibrates on 5–8 real profiles.

**What happens to people who miss a must-have?**
Anyone who clearly lacks one ranks below everyone who doesn’t. Nobody is hidden.

**Where does Buddy find people?**
In your talent pool, by plain-English search; in similar companies Buddy profiles for you, each with source links; in new people found every day for each open role; and among past candidates who nearly made it.

**What is the timing score?**
It shows when it’s a good moment to reach out: time in the same title from the CV, company news such as layoffs or leadership changes with a source link, and public profile updates.

**How does screening work?**
Every criterion is checked against the CV with a quoted line, plus a profile score and a talent grade. You review one candidate at a time — shortlist, hold, or reject with a reason.

**Do messages go out automatically?**
No. Buddy drafts personal messages from real profile facts, and nothing is sent until you approve it. Candidates confirm interest in one click and book their own interview slot.

**What does the call check do?**
Working with your notetaker, Buddy checks each interview call against the JD both ways — did the candidate match it, and did the interviewer ask about it — and flags questions on personal or protected topics.

**How do you check answers are genuine?**
Buddy checks for timeline, specificity and copied or AI-generated text, near-identical CVs and CV-versus-call mismatches. It never gives a verdict — it asks a person to check.

**Do you replace my recruiters or hiring managers?**
No. Buddy finds and checks. People decide.

**What does it cost?**
A percentage of CTC, charged only when you hire.

## Role insights

- **Skills and criteria**: The JD broken into the skills and criteria a candidate is measured against.
- **A ready search query**: A search string, ready to run, built from the criteria.
- **Wording check**: Flags vague or exclusionary wording in the JD before the search starts.
- **Must-haves**: Mark the non-negotiables. Anyone who clearly lacks one ranks below everyone who doesn’t. Nobody is hidden.
- **Calibration**: The hiring manager reacts to 5–8 real profiles and says why; Buddy suggests sharper criteria from the reasons.
- **Hiring memory**: Buddy remembers what each company’s hiring managers wanted, so the next role starts sharper.
- **Salary benchmarks** (coming soon): Market ranges for the role.

## Evidence-based screening

- **A quote for every criterion**: Each criterion is checked against the CV, with the line that proves it.
- **Essentials met**: How many of the role’s essentials the profile matches — “Matches 4 of 5 essentials”.
- **Profile score**: A score out of 100 against the role.
- **Talent grade**: A+, A, B or C — from the score, never instead of it.
- **Review mode**: One candidate at a time. S to shortlist, H to hold, R to reject — a reason is required.
- **Past candidates**: People who nearly made it for other roles come back for another look.

Talent grades: A+ 95–100, A 80–94, B 65–79, C Under 65. A grade never rejects anyone.

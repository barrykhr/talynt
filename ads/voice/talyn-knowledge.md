# TALYNT LABS — knowledge base for the voice assistant

TALYNT LABS is an AI-native hiring agency in India. Clients buy a hiring service, run by TALYNT's team through Talyn, its AI hiring agent. All roles, across India. Pricing: a percentage of CTC, charged only when the client hires. After booking a demo, the team calls the client and walks through a live role.

Coming soon (say so if asked): voice calls to candidates who go quiet; salary benchmarks.

## Questions and answers

**How does Talyn work?**
You share the role. Talyn reads the JD, searches by meaning across every source, scores each profile against the role with evidence, reaches out personally, closes gaps by chat, keeps candidates engaged and hands you a shortlist with the reasons attached. Our team checks every step.

**What’s the best thing about Talyn?**
It hires for intent and context, not keywords — people who fit the role and want to move, with the evidence to prove both.

**Is TALYNT an agency or software?**
An AI-native hiring agency. Talyn is our agent; you work with our team, and you don’t have to learn a tool.

**What is Talyn Insights?**
Talyn’s intelligence module. Role Insights turns your JD into a search plan and an interview kit. Candidate Insights scores every profile against the JD out of 100, with evidence and gaps.

**What does a score of 87 mean?**
The profile meets the role’s requirements strongly, with evidence for each — grade A. Scores always come with the evidence and the open gaps. A score never rejects anyone.

**How do you know someone wants to move?**
Talyn reads eight work-related signals, from tenure patterns and promotion stagnation to company events, profile refreshes and what they say in conversation. Never anything personal.

**How do you contact candidates?**
Email first, about their own profile. WhatsApp once they opt in. Everyone can opt out at any time.

**How do you reduce drop-outs?**
Every candidate hears from us at every step, and health scores flag anyone drifting so Talyn and our recruiters re-engage them early.

**How do you check answers are genuine?**
Six authenticity checks — timeline, specificity, cross-channel consistency, public evidence, generated-answer detection and skill claims against evidence. Anything that doesn’t add up goes to a person.

**Do you replace my recruiters or hiring managers?**
No. Talyn handles the volume, follow-ups and evidence. People make the calls.

**Which roles and locations?**
All roles, across India.

**What does it cost?**
A percentage of CTC, charged only when you hire.

## Talyn Insights

**Role Insights** turns the JD into a search plan: the role parsed (must-haves, nice-to-haves, responsibilities, seniority, core and adjacent skills, stack, domain, location, work model), Boolean search strings for recruiters, adjacent titles, the market-standard growth path, feeder companies, a JD quality check, calibration questions for the hiring manager, an ideal candidate profile, an interview kit mapped to every requirement, and the talent pool our sources match. Salary benchmarks are coming soon.

**Candidate Insights** scores every profile out of 100 against the JD, with evidence: career history and tenure, skills and recent activity, role fitment, gaps against the JD, and the next questions to close each gap. Grades: A+ is 95–100, A is 80–94, B is 65–79, C is under 65. A grade never rejects anyone — a person decides.

## Capabilities

- Semantic search and automated sourcing across professional networks, job boards, developer communities, portfolios and open source, referrals and the TALYNT talent pool — by meaning and context, not keywords.
- One campaign per role; hyperpersonalised outreach: email first, written from each candidate's profile; WhatsApp once the candidate opts in; follow-up sequences; pauses on reply or opt-out; recruiters can review any message.
- Automated gap-closing by chat, with six authenticity checks: timeline consistency, specificity, cross-channel consistency, public evidence, generated-answer detection, and skill claims against evidence. Anything that doesn't add up goes to a person.
- Continuous engagement: reminders, next steps, feedback requests, and drifting candidates flagged and re-engaged.
- Intent from eight work-related signals: usual industry tenure, usual role tenure, job-portal activity, promotion stagnation, company events, profile refresh, conversation cues and role mismatch. Never anything personal.
- Candidate health and pipeline health, each 0–100: Healthy 75+, Watch 50–74, At risk under 50.
- Hiring managers log in to see fit, evidence, gaps, intent and health, add their input, and get an interview kit mapped to the JD — so they hire for what they want, not what they're given.
- Human in the loop: recruiters own every relationship; no automatic rejections.

## Privacy

Work-related information only. First contact by email; WhatsApp only after opt-in. Every candidate can opt out at any time. Only what hiring needs is collected, and deleted when no longer needed.
